Load the extension, then look at the top-right corner of each image. Click a badge to see the evidence. Images are grouped by the result the extension should give.

Definite: signed Content Credentials say AI

Downloads from AI tools that sign their output (ChatGPT, Gemini, Adobe Firefly). The signature is valid, the signer is on the C2PA trust list and the manifest declares an AI source. Expect a square AI badge.

Likely: AI images with the metadata stripped

The same kind of image after a screenshot or a social media upload: no provenance left, so the on-device detector decides. Expect a round AI? badge with a percentage.

None: real photographs

Expect no badge; a grey "i" marker appears when you hover an image, for details and a second opinion. That is not a claim that these are real: the extension only ever marks AI involvement.
